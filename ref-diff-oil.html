Rear Differential Oil — Full Specifications

2009 DB9 Coupé · 5.9L V12Oil change at 64,098 miles · October 2026← All reference topics

The Touchtronic DB9's Graziano AM803D limited-slip differential has its own gear-oil sump. It is not shared with the ZF 6HP26 gearbox ATF (manual and Sportshift cars are different: their one-piece AM803T transaxle shares gear oil between gearbox and diff). The factory oil is near-unobtainable in the UK, so this section sets out what the diff actually needs and how the common alternatives measure up. It's based on the oil change done at 64,098 miles in October 2026 — see the log.

What the oil must meet

  • SAE 75W-90 — the "90" grade means 13.5 to <18.5 cSt at 100 °C (SAE J306). The 75W rating covers cold flow
  • API GL-5 — the essential one. The AM803D has a hypoid crown wheel and pinion behind 570 Nm of V12, and hypoid teeth slide hard under load. GL-5 carries roughly twice the sulphur-phosphorus extreme-pressure additive of GL-4
  • API MT-1 — thermal stability, seal compatibility and resistance to copper corrosion under sustained heat. Desirable, not essential on a road car
  • LS — friction modifier for the limited-slip clutch packs. Without it, the diff judders or chatters on tight, cold turns. If the oil isn't LS-rated, add it separately
⚠ GL-4 and "GL4+" are manual-gearbox ratings. These oils keep extreme-pressure additive low to protect brass synchro rings. A diff has no synchros, so that compromise gains nothing and leaves the hypoid set under-protected. "GL4+" is a marketing term, not an API category.

Limited-slip additive

  • — Aston and dealer practice is to add LSD friction modifier (traditionally Kendall) even with the factory Shell oil. That's because AXRME is a heavy-duty truck axle oil with no LS rating
  • — Used here: Westway Lubricants LSD additive, 150 ml bottle, dosed at 40 ml per litre. Pre-mixed into each 1 L bottle and shaken before filling
  • — With an LS-rated oil (Motul Gear 300 LS, Westway LSD oil), extra additive is optional. Only add it if judder shows up on tight, cold turns
  • — The additive acts on the clutch plates (stick-slip). It doesn't reduce viscous drag or replace GL-5 or MT-1 protection
ℹ Our choice, October 2026: Comma SX75W-90 GL-5 + Westway additive at 40 ml/L. It meets GL-5, its viscosity is almost identical to the factory oil, and it was on the shelf the same day. It's semi-synthetic with no MT-1, so treat it as an interim fill: we'll change it at 12 months (October 2027), probably to Motul Gear 300 LS 75W-90.
