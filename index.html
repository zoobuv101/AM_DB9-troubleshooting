V12 Post-Service Misfire — Blocked Injector After Ultrasonic Cleaning April 2026
After a full plug and coilpack replacement (both manifolds off, all 12 injectors out and ultrasonically cleaned), the engine returned to running with misfires on the passenger bank only. Seven diagnostic stages traced it to a single injector blocked by debris that resettled after cleaning — electrically perfect, invisible to the ECU, delivering no fuel. Includes the twin-ECU cylinder mapping diagram, the P0305 ghost-code explanation, and the coast-down relearn procedure.
